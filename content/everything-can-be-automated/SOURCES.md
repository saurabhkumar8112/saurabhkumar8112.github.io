# Sources and illustration notes

## Historical references

- Jacquard loom: Joseph Marie Jacquard demonstrated his punch card controlled loom in 1801. Standard histories of computing and textile technology cover it.
- Ford moving assembly line: introduced at Highland Park in 1913. The commonly cited figures are a drop in Model T chassis assembly time from roughly twelve hours to about ninety minutes. See the Henry Ford Museum and Ford Motor Company historical materials.
